## 25 aturan grammar yang paling sering diuji

Aturan-aturan ini mencakup sebagian besar soal structure dan written expression di TOEFL ITP, ELPT, AcEPT, dan SIMAK. Pelajari satu sampai tiga aturan per hari, lalu langsung kerjakan latihan kilat structure.

### 1. Setiap kalimat butuh subjek dan verb utama
- ✓ *The results of the study **were** published last year.*
- ✗ *The results of the study published last year.* (tanpa verb utama, artinya berubah)
- Jebakan: kata berakhiran -ing atau to + verb **bukan** verb utama.

### 2. Subjek dan verb harus sepadan
- Abaikan frasa di antara subjek dan verb: *The quality of the essays **is** good.*
- Frasa *together with, as well as, along with* tidak mengubah subjek: *The professor, together with his assistants, **is** here.*

### 3. Subjek khusus
- *The number of* + jamak → tunggal; *a number of* + jamak → jamak.
- *Each, every, everyone, nobody* → tunggal.
- Bidang ilmu berakhiran -s (*mathematics, economics, physics*) dan *news* → tunggal.
- *Police, people* → jamak. *Data* dalam TOEFL diperlakukan jamak.
- Jarak, uang, waktu sebagai satu kesatuan → tunggal: *Ten kilometers **is** a long way.*

### 4. Penanda tenses
| Penanda | Tenses |
|---|---|
| yesterday, ago, in 2019 | simple past |
| since + titik waktu, for + durasi, already, yet | present perfect |
| by the time + past, before + past | past perfect |
| by + waktu masa depan | future perfect |
| when + past (sedang berlangsung lalu terpotong) | past continuous |

### 5. Kalimat pengandaian
| Tipe | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (mungkin) | If + present, will + V | If you study, you will pass. |
| 2 (tidak nyata sekarang) | If + past, would + V | If I had time, I would join. |
| 3 (tidak nyata masa lalu) | If + had V3, would have V3 | If I had known, I would have applied. |
- Inversi: *Had I known* = If I had known; *Should you need* = If you need; *Were I* = If I were.

### 6. Kalimat pasif
- Pola: be + V3. *The data **were collected** from 500 respondents.*
- Tanya: apakah subjek melakukan pekerjaan? Jika tidak, pakai pasif.

### 7. Gerund atau infinitive
- + V-ing: *enjoy, avoid, finish, consider, suggest, mind, keep, deny*, dan semua **preposisi** (*interested in learning*).
- + to V: *want, plan, decide, hope, agree, refuse, need, afford*.
- *Be used to + V-ing* (terbiasa) ≠ *used to + V* (dulu biasa).

### 8. Kausatif
- *have someone do*: *She had her assistant **check** the data.*
- *get someone to do*: *He got his friend **to proofread** the essay.*
- *have something done*: *I had my documents **translated**.*

### 9. Subjunctive
- Setelah *suggest, recommend, insist, demand, require that* dan *It is essential/important/necessary that* → verb bentuk dasar tanpa -s.
- ✓ *It is essential that every applicant **submit** the form.*

### 10. Klausa relatif
- *who* (orang, subjek), *whom* (orang, objek), *whose* (milik), *which* (benda), *that* (orang/benda, tanpa koma).
- Klausa yang diapit koma **tidak boleh** memakai *that*: *Coffee, **which** originated in Ethiopia, …*

### 11. Klausa yang diringkas (participle)
- Aktif → V-ing: *Students **living** on campus …* (= who live)
- Pasif → V3: *The temple, **built** in the 9th century, …* (= which was built)

### 12. Pertanyaan tidak langsung
- Di dalam kalimat, urutan kata kembali seperti pernyataan.
- ✓ *She asked me where **I studied**.* ✗ *where did I study*

### 13. Konjungsi vs preposisi
| Diikuti klausa (S + V) | Diikuti frasa benda |
|---|---|
| although, even though, though | despite, in spite of |
| because, since, as | because of, due to |
| while, when | during |

### 14. Pasangan konjungsi
- *both … and, either … or, neither … nor, not only … but also, whether … or*.
- Dengan *either/neither … or/nor*, verb mengikuti subjek terdekat.

### 15. Paralelisme
- Unsur dalam daftar harus berbentuk sama: *reading, writing, and **speaking*** (bukan *to speak*).
- ✓ *The lecturer explained the theory clearly, concisely, and thoroughly.*

### 16. Inversi setelah kata negatif
- *Never, rarely, seldom, hardly … when, no sooner … than, not until, only after, not only* di awal kalimat → auxiliary + subjek.
- ✓ *Rarely **have scientists** seen such results.*

### 17. Perbandingan
- Satu suku kata: *-er than / the -est*. Panjang: *more … than / the most*.
- Jangan dobel: ✗ *more smarter*.
- *As … as* untuk setara; *the more …, the more …* untuk perbandingan berganda.
- Bandingkan hal yang setara: *The weather in Bandung is cooler than **that of** Jakarta.*

### 18. Adjective vs adverb
- Adverb menerangkan verb, adjective, atau adverb lain: *grew **rapidly***.
- Setelah linking verb (*be, seem, look, feel, become, sound, taste*) gunakan adjective: *The plan **seems reasonable**.*

### 19. Adjective -ing vs -ed
- -ing = penyebab perasaan: *a **boring** lecture*.
- -ed = yang merasakan: *the **bored** students*.

### 20. Dapat dihitung vs tidak dapat dihitung
| Dapat dihitung | Tidak dapat dihitung |
|---|---|
| many, few, a few, fewer, number of | much, little, a little, less, amount of |
- Tidak dapat dihitung dan tidak dijamakkan: *information, advice, equipment, research, knowledge, evidence*.

### 21. Artikel
- *a* + bunyi konsonan, *an* + bunyi vokal (*an hour, a university*).
- *the* untuk yang sudah jelas, superlatif, urutan (*the first*), dan benda unik (*the sun*).

### 22. Kata ganti
- Kata ganti harus sesuai dengan yang dirujuk (tunggal/jamak).
- *its* (milik) ≠ *it's* (it is). *whose* ≠ *who's*.
- *Who* sebagai subjek, *whom* sebagai objek: *the researcher **whom** we interviewed*.

### 23. Preposisi dan kolokasi
| Pasangan tetap | |
|---|---|
| interested in, responsible for, depend on, consist of | different from, similar to, aware of, capable of |
| in + bulan/tahun, on + hari/tanggal, at + jam | no later than, according to, in spite of |

### 24. Redundansi dan urutan kata
- Hindari pengulangan makna: ✗ *return back, repeat again, the reason is because, discuss about*.
- Urutan kata sifat: opini → ukuran → umur → bentuk → warna → asal → bahan.

### 25. Kata yang sering tertukar
| Kata | Arti |
|---|---|
| then / than | lalu / daripada |
| affect / effect | memengaruhi (v) / pengaruh (n) |
| rise / raise | naik (tanpa objek) / menaikkan (dengan objek) |
| lie / lay | berbaring / meletakkan |
| principal / principle | kepala sekolah, utama / prinsip |
| economic / economical | terkait ekonomi / hemat |
| fewer / less | untuk benda terhitung / tak terhitung |
